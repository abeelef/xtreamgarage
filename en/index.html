Your questions answered

Frequently asked questions

How long does a service take?

Timing depends on the service. Window tinting or basic cleaning may take a couple of hours, while ceramic coatings, paint correction or PPF installation can take one to several days at our workshop to allow proper drying and curing.

Will tinted windows cause problems at the Spanish vehicle inspection (ITV)?

No. All our Premium and Nanoceramic window films are approved for use on Spanish roads. We provide a written approval certificate and apply the required markings to each window.

Do ceramic coatings protect against scratches?

Ceramic coatings provide resistance to light surface scratches, chemicals such as bird droppings or resin, and UV radiation, as well as excellent water repellency. They do not protect against stone chips or heavy scuffs. For that level of physical protection, we recommend PPF.
